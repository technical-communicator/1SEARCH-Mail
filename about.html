About

Our mission

The mission of Search Central Florida is to highlight the amazing arts and culture that is happening every day across Central Florida.

We started Search Central Florida on Instagram with the goal of curating some of the best events in Central Florida. This website is an extension of that mission: to keep spreading and sharing the amazing things that go on in Central Florida.

Good to know

What is Search Central Florida?

A hyperlocal guide to the arts, culture and small businesses of Orlando and Central Florida, run by Halley and Zach.

How do you choose events?

We curate what we think our community will love. Every event is checked by a real person before it's listed.

Can I add my event?

Yes! Submit it here. It's free and takes about two minutes.

What's the snail mail club?

A monthly package of art from a local artist, plus a perk from a local business. See the tiers.

How can I reach you?

DM us on Instagram @searchcentralfl or email searchcentralflorida@gmail.com.
