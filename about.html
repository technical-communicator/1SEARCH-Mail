About SCF

A hyperlocal guide to Orlando arts, culture and small businesses.

Search Central Florida is a hyperlocal guide to Orlando arts, culture and small businesses.

We share a handful of hand-picked events every day on Instagram, and this site is where even more good events live.

We believe the best stuff happens in person. Find something fun, close the app and go.
