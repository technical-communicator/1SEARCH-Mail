Why we do this

The mission of Search Central Florida is to highlight the amazing arts and culture that is happening every day across Central Florida.

We started Search Central Florida on Instagram with the goal of curating some of the best events in Central Florida. This website is an extension of that mission: to keep spreading and sharing the amazing things that go on in Central Florida.
