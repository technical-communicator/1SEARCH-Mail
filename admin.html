Review events

Check submitted events, fix anything that needs it, then approve or reject. Approved events show on the calendar right away.

Logging in isn't switched on yet. The login below starts working once the site is connected to Supabase, where your login and the submitted events are stored. It's a one-time setup of about 30 minutes:

  1. Create a free Supabase project and run supabase/schema.sql (with your email in it).
  2. Create your login in Supabase (Authentication → Users), with that same email and a password.
  3. Set up the spam check, add the two server functions and their secret keys.
  4. Paste the three public values into config.js.

Every step is explained in plain language in the setup guide (README). After that, come back here and log in with your email and password.
