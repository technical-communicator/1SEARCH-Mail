hi friends!! we are so happy to announce that november's featured artist of the month is Xine Hann!! Xine is a pillar of the creative community here in Central Florida as the founder/ director of [Orlando Collage Club](https://www.instagram.com/orlandocollageclub/)! Crazy! I know! You may have even been to one of their events already! If not, definitely check one out! Here is a bit more about Xine Hann:

> Hi, my name is Xine. I am a mom and graphic designer in Central Florida.
>
> After earning a degree from Stetson University, I travelled to New Jersey and then Hawaii, learning tattoo design, zines, block printing, and a little about the wood arts.
>
> Over the past several years, I’ve focused on improving my collage skills, developing my own style of 3D collage artwork.
>
> Recently, I started the Orlando Collage Club to share this passion with others. If you’re curious to know more about Orlando Collage Club, you can find us on Instagram and Facebook [@OrlandoCollageClub](https://www.instagram.com/orlandocollageclub/). You can also find more of my work on Instagram [@Xine_Hann](https://www.instagram.com/xine_hann/).
