## What to expect at this year's Florida Coffee Festival

Here is what you can expect at this year's Florida Coffee Festival on November 15th:

The third annual Florida Coffee Festival is back again at Festival Park! Each coffee booth will have a sample ready for you to taste your way through an incredible lineup of local coffee shops, roasters, matcha brands, and specialty beverage companies from across Florida and beyond. This event is curated for those chasing the perfect espresso, looking to discover a new matcha, or hunting for a locally crafted mug!

Come discover your next favorite coffee shop, meet the people behind your favorite brands, and celebrate the community that makes up Florida’s coffee scene!

Expect to enjoy:

* Coffee and matcha samples from some of the best brands and cafés around.
* Espressos, pour overs, cold brews, matcha lattes, and specialty drinks.
* Interactive experiences, like coffee cuppings and goat cuddling.
* Live competitions, including the Florida Latte Art Championship.
* Live music, and local artist and chef pop-ups.
* Exclusive merch and festival releases.
