Frequently Asked Questions

What's in each mailer?

it depends on your tier: two stickers, a 4x6 art print, or both, from that month's featured central florida artist. every tier also gets a discount to a local business and the @searchcentralfl newsletter.

How much does it cost?

$6.99, $8.99 or $12.99 a month, depending on your tier.

When will my first package arrive?

sign up by the last sunday of the month and your first package ships during the first week of the following month. it usually arrives within 5 to 7 business days after that.

Can I track my package?

no. everything travels via USPS in untracked envelopes, so keep an eye on your mailbox during the delivery window.

How do I cancel?

you can cancel any time on Patreon, no questions asked.
