Local artists & local perks

A new local artist every month

every month we feature a different central florida artist. we spotlight their work, their story, and their place in the community.

each artist receives a stipend to support their creative journey.

$450 given directly to local artists so far, and counting!

Perks from local businesses

alongside local art, we include discounts from local creative businesses we love, so shopping local becomes part of your monthly routine. businesses who have graciously given special perks to our members so far:

  • Diablicos Coffee
  • Funk's Vintage
  • Timucua Arts
  • Mead Botanical Garden
  • Authentic Books

with more added every month.

How it works

  1. sign up by the last sunday of the month.
  2. your first package ships during the first week of the following month.
  3. check your mailbox. everything travels via USPS in untracked envelopes, so expect it within 5 to 7 business days and keep an eye on your mail during that window.
  4. keep collecting. you're billed monthly and your package arrives regularly. cancel any time, no questions asked.

Why a snail mail club?

the best things don't live on a screen. we created SEARCH: a snail mail club for people who believe in supporting local artists directly, who want to discover central florida beyond the tourist traps, and who still think there's something special about receiving snail mail.

Questions

What's in each mailer?

it depends on your tier: a sticker, a 4x6 art print, or both, from that month's featured central florida artist. every tier also gets a discount to a local business and the @searchcentralfl newsletter.

How much does it cost?

$6.99, $8.99 or $12.99 a month, depending on your tier.

When will my first package arrive?

sign up by the last sunday of the month and your first package ships during the first week of the following month. it usually arrives within 5 to 7 business days after that.

Can I track my package?

no. everything travels via USPS in untracked envelopes, so keep an eye on your mailbox during the delivery window.

How do I cancel?

you can cancel any time on Patreon, no questions asked.
